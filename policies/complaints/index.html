Complaint & Dispute Resolution Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

All Motion Sports Association is committed to providing athletes, parents/guardians, coaches, volunteers, and other participants with a respectful, safe, fair, and transparent process for raising concerns.

Informal Resolution

Whenever appropriate, individuals are encouraged to first attempt to resolve routine concerns respectfully with the relevant coach or club representative.

Formal Complaints

If the matter cannot reasonably be resolved informally, or if the matter is serious, a formal complaint may be submitted in writing.

The complaint should include:

  • Name and contact information of the complainant
  • Name of the person(s) involved
  • Description of the incident or concern
  • Relevant dates and locations
  • Any supporting information or documentation
  • The resolution being requested, if applicable

Impartiality

Complaints will be handled as fairly and impartially as reasonably possible.

A person who is directly involved in the complaint should not be responsible for making the final decision concerning that complaint.

Independent Complaints Contact

All Motion Sports is currently in the process of appointing an independent complaints contact.

Status

Independent Complaints Contact – Currently being appointed.

Once appointed, the individual's contact information will be published on this page.

The purpose of this role is to provide participants with an additional and reasonably independent point of contact, particularly when a complaint concerns the Club Director, Head Coach, another coach, or another person in a position of authority.

Until the appointment is finalized, complaints may be submitted through the club's official contact channel. Where a conflict of interest exists, the club will take reasonable steps to have the matter reviewed by an appropriate independent person.

Confidentiality

Information relating to complaints will be shared only with individuals who reasonably need the information to assess, investigate, or resolve the matter, subject to legal, safety, safeguarding, insurance, or governing-body reporting requirements.

Non-Retaliation

All Motion Sports prohibits retaliation against an individual who raises a concern or participates in a complaint process in good faith.

Serious Safety or Misconduct Concerns

Matters involving abuse, harassment, discrimination, threats, serious misconduct, or athlete safety may be referred to the appropriate governing body, independent authority, law enforcement, child protection authority, or other organization when required or appropriate.

Volleyball BC

All Motion Sports will cooperate with applicable Volleyball BC and Volleyball Canada policies, reporting requirements, disciplinary processes, and sanctions.
