Financial, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

All Motion Sports Association is committed to maintaining clear and transparent financial practices for athletes and families.

Payment

Program fees must be paid according to the payment schedule communicated during registration.

Where offered, families may choose between full payment and an approved installment/payment plan.

Any applicable registration, third-party processing, Volleyball BC, Volleyball Canada, tournament, or membership fees may be identified separately during registration.

Cancellation by Participant

Requests to withdraw from a program must be submitted in writing to All Motion Sports.

For seasonal programs:

  • Before the program begins: eligible program fees may be refunded, less any non-refundable administrative, registration, processing, membership, or third-party fees already incurred by the club.
  • Before 50% of the program has been completed: the participant may receive a prorated refund for eligible remaining program fees.
  • After 50% of the program has been completed: program fees are normally non-refundable.

Medical Withdrawal

If an athlete cannot continue participating because of injury or illness, the club may provide a prorated refund or credit for the remaining eligible portion of the program.

All Motion Sports may request appropriate medical documentation when necessary.

Club Cancellations

If All Motion Sports cancels a session, the club will make reasonable efforts to provide a replacement session, credit, or appropriate prorated refund.

Sessions cancelled because of circumstances outside the club's reasonable control, including facility closures, severe weather, emergencies, or government restrictions, will be handled reasonably based on the circumstances and costs already incurred.

Non-Refundable Costs

Third-party costs already incurred on behalf of an athlete may be non-refundable. These may include registration fees, governing-body membership fees, tournament fees, uniforms or merchandise already ordered, online processing fees, and similar committed expenses.

Fines and Financial Penalties

Any fine or financial penalty charged directly to an athlete or family must have a reasonable basis and must be communicated transparently.

All Motion Sports will not impose arbitrary or undisclosed financial penalties.

Questions concerning payments or refunds should be directed to All Motion Sports through the club's official contact information.
