Athlete Code of Conduct

Last Updated: August 2026

Athletes representing All Motion Sports are expected to demonstrate respect, responsibility, effort, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

Athletes agree to:

  • Treat teammates, coaches, opponents, officials, volunteers, parents, and facility staff with respect.
  • Participate without discrimination, harassment, bullying, intimidation, or abusive behaviour.
  • Use appropriate and respectful language.
  • Arrive on time and prepared for practices, games, tournaments, and team activities.
  • Listen to coaching instructions and make a genuine effort to learn and improve.
  • Support teammates regardless of ability, experience, background, or role on the team.
  • Demonstrate good sportsmanship in victory and defeat.
  • Respect officials and their decisions.
  • Care for volleyball equipment and facilities.
  • Follow reasonable safety instructions.
  • Inform a coach when injured or unable to participate safely.
  • Represent All Motion Sports appropriately during competitions and club activities.
  • Use social media responsibly and refrain from online bullying, harassment, threats, or inappropriate content involving teammates, coaches, opponents, or the club.
  • Follow applicable Volleyball BC and Volleyball Canada rules and policies.

All Motion Sports believes athlete development includes more than volleyball performance. The club seeks to help young athletes develop confidence, discipline, responsibility, resilience, teamwork, leadership, respect, and positive lifelong habits through sport.

Breaches of this Code may result in appropriate corrective or disciplinary action depending on the circumstances and seriousness of the conduct.
