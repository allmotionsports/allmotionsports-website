Discipline, Reporting & Governing Body Compliance

Last Updated: August 2026

All Motion Sports Association is committed to complying with applicable Volleyball BC and Volleyball Canada rules, policies, registration requirements, coach eligibility requirements, competition requirements, and disciplinary processes.

All Motion Sports will:

  • Take reasonable steps to address breaches of club policies.
  • Apply disciplinary measures proportionately and fairly.
  • Provide an individual with a reasonable opportunity to understand and respond to allegations when appropriate.
  • Maintain appropriate records of serious disciplinary matters.
  • Inform Volleyball BC in writing of disciplinary sanctions imposed against Volleyball BC registered individuals when required by Volleyball BC.
  • Recognize and uphold applicable disciplinary sanctions communicated by Volleyball BC or Volleyball Canada.
  • Cooperate with governing-body investigations and processes when required.

Disciplinary action may include a warning, behavioural expectations, temporary restriction, suspension, removal from an activity, or termination of participation or club involvement, depending on the seriousness and circumstances of the conduct.

Nothing in this policy prevents All Motion Sports from taking immediate reasonable action when necessary to protect athlete safety.
