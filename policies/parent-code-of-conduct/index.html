Parent/Guardian Code of Conduct

Last Updated: August 2026

Parents and guardians play an important role in creating a positive environment for young athletes.

Parents/guardians agree to:

  • Treat athletes, coaches, officials, volunteers, opponents, other parents, and facility staff respectfully.
  • Encourage effort, improvement, teamwork, and sportsmanship.
  • Avoid abusive, threatening, discriminatory, harassing, or intimidating behaviour.
  • Refrain from coaching athletes from the sidelines when doing so conflicts with team coaching.
  • Respect reasonable coaching and team decisions.
  • Allow coaches to provide technical instruction during practices and competition.
  • Communicate concerns respectfully and through appropriate club channels.
  • Avoid confrontational discussions with coaches immediately before, during, or immediately after games or practices.
  • Respect officials and refrain from abusive criticism of officiating.
  • Support a positive environment for all athletes, not only their own child.
  • Respect athlete privacy and the privacy of other families.
  • Follow facility rules and applicable Volleyball BC and Volleyball Canada policies.
  • Inform the club of relevant information necessary for the athlete's safe participation.

Parents are encouraged to raise legitimate concerns. Raising a concern respectfully and in good faith will not negatively affect an athlete's standing within the club.
