Conflict of Interest Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

All Motion Sports Association is committed to making decisions fairly and in the best interests of the club and its participants.

A conflict of interest may exist when a director, coach, administrator, volunteer, or other decision-maker has a personal, family, professional, or financial interest that could improperly influence, or reasonably appear to influence, a club decision.

Disclosure

Individuals must disclose an actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest when they become aware of it.

Examples may include:

  • Decisions directly affecting a family member
  • Personal financial interests
  • Business relationships
  • Selection or disciplinary decisions involving close personal relationships
  • Situations where personal interests could interfere with impartial decision-making

Managing a Conflict

Depending on the circumstances, the individual may be required to:

  • Disclose the conflict
  • Refrain from participating in discussion
  • Refrain from voting or making the decision
  • Allow another appropriate and impartial person to handle the matter

The existence of a relationship does not automatically mean misconduct has occurred. The purpose of this policy is to ensure transparency and fair decision-making.

Complaints involving an individual with a conflict of interest should be handled by another appropriate person whenever reasonably possible.
