2027 team structure

Two Pathways. One Club. One Community.

Our goal for the 2027 season is to establish up to two teams per age division.

Competitive team

All Motion Black

All Motion Black is designed for athletes who demonstrate strong volleyball fundamentals, game understanding, teamwork, commitment, and readiness for a competitive environment.

The program will focus on:

  • Technical and tactical development.
  • Competitive performance.
  • Game understanding and decision-making.
  • Teamwork and communication.
  • Tournament preparation.

Competitive development team

All Motion White

All Motion White is designed for athletes who are continuing to develop their volleyball skills while gaining valuable competitive experience.

The program will focus on:

  • Technical improvement.
  • Game understanding.
  • Confidence and consistency.
  • Teamwork and communication.
  • Competitive growth.

Both Teams Will Compete

Our intention is for both All Motion Black and All Motion White to participate in the Volleyball BC competitive season, including appropriate tournaments and provincial championships where eligible.

Both teams will have opportunities to train, compete, develop, and represent All Motion Sports.

The difference between the teams is the athletes' current level of development, not their opportunity to compete.

Teams may compete in different divisions or tiers depending on tournament structures and results.

Team Formation

As a new and growing volleyball club, our goal is to establish up to two teams per age division.

The final number of teams will depend on athlete interest, tryout participation, skill levels, coaching availability, and overall team readiness.

Team formation and placement will be confirmed following tryouts.

U16 remains pending confirmation.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my daughter try out without previous club experience?

Yes. We welcome athletes with different volleyball backgrounds. Team selection will depend on skills, potential, commitment, and overall readiness.

Will All Motion Sports have two teams per age division?

Our goal is to form up to two teams per age division: Black and White. Final team numbers will depend on tryout participation, athlete readiness, and available resources.

Will both Black and White participate in competitions?

Yes! All Motion Sports is ready to compete in the 2027 Volleyball BC competitive season. Both our Black and White teams will be built with the goal of participating in official tournaments and provincial championships, where eligible.

We are committed to providing our athletes with a competitive volleyball experience, regardless of which team they join.

Our ultimate goal is to compete at the 2027 Volleyball Canada National Championships.

Does registering guarantee a place on a team?

No. Tryout registration provides an opportunity to be evaluated. Team offers will be made following the selection process.

What if my daughter is not selected?

Athletes who are not selected may have opportunities to continue developing through our regular All Motion Sports training programs.

Is U16 confirmed?

Not yet. The U16 program and tryout date are pending confirmation.

How much does registration cost?

$40 CAD per athlete.
